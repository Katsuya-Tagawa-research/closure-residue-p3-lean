## A. A mathematically typed target for the generative notation

The conceptual shorthand `F: ∅ → (Λ, Ω)` is not an ordinary function *whose value on an element of the empty set* is `(Λ, Ω)`: the empty domain contains no input. In **Set**, the unique map from the initial object `∅` to a nonempty set is the empty function. A mathematical reconstruction of the intended generative assertion requires an explicit kind of data/logic: e.g. a judgment `⊢_F (Λ, Ω)`, an initial-algebra construction, a rule on presented generators/relations, or a functor from a specified category of premises. These are proposed formalization routes, not yet established equivalent formulations of the original philosophical F.

For a candidate category `CRSrc` of *physically sourced* finite CR models, specifying just `Hilbert dimension, CPTP channel, POVM, instrument, alphabet` is not enough to guarantee connection to original P3: define the original P3 hidden filtration, signed quadratic residue, allowed morphisms, gauge/orientation group and independent native-source provenance **before** claiming an F-specific theorem. An observation-data functor `U: CRSrc → OpLang` can then forget microscopic details. The `OpLang` category of protocols and its morphisms must be specified independently of desired reconstruction.

## B. Relative operational reconstruction: exact positive theorem

Fix a **known labeled Hilbert-space matrix representation** `M_d(C)`, an independently calibrated real basis of `d²` preparation states `ρ_i`, an independently calibrated spanning family of readout functionals `Tr[M_j·]`, and a **marked** finite instrument alphabet. For each marked branch CP map `I_a`, obtain all `d⁴` real numbers

    D^(a)_{ji} = Tr[M_j I_a(ρ_i)].

**Theorem.** If preparations and readout functionals both span `Herm_d` over `R`, the numbers determine `I_a` uniquely as a Hermiticity-preserving linear map. In a chosen basis `B` of Hermitian matrices, write preparation coordinate matrix `S` and readout matrix `M`. Then

    D^(a)=M R_a S,     R_a=M^(-1) D^(a) S^(-1).

The coefficient map is the invertible Kronecker matrix `S^T ⊗ M` of rank `d⁴`. This is standard process tomography, **conditional on actual independent frame calibration**. It does not imply absence of unknown native labels, uniqueness of a Stinespring/environment implementation, or derivation of the frame from F.

## D. Exact independence and non-selection witnesses

**Unmarked port:** Two formal completions share a baseline and the complete marked `X` control-word subalphabet. Model 1's declared native span is `{X}` (real dimension 1), model 2 has `{X,Z}` (dimension 2). All marked data agree, so A-FRAME for those marked operations does not prove A-INVENTORY. This is a model-completion counterexample, not two observed real devices.

**Inaccessible environment:** For A and B qubits, let `p_a` be computational A projectors. The two instruments with branch Kraus `p_a ⊗ I_B` versus `p_a ⊗ Z_B` have identical reduced A instrument maps for **every** joint input and word; `Tr_B[(p_a⊗U)R(p_a⊗U†)]=p_a(Tr_B R)p_a`. Yet their nonselective channel superoperator traces are **8** versus **0**, so they are not even linearly similar. An independently prepared B in `X+` with B-X readout differentiates expectation **+1** vs **−1**. This makes A-ENVIRONMENT independent of A-only maximal data.

**Orientation:** Under AX11's simultaneous transpose companion, all internal words remain equal. A fixed externally calibrated `Y+` probe gives the tetrahedral `M_0` outcome difference `1/(2√3)` between the oriented and transposed frames. Independent phase standard lifts this *particular* ambiguity; it does not establish source exhaustiveness or F.

**No natural symmetric selector without a symmetry-breaking source:** A 2-element source fiber `{+,−}` with a swap action projects to one observation with a trivial symmetry action. No equivariant section exists: a selected source would need to be fixed by the swap, which neither fiber element is. This is a finite group-action selection lemma, **only an analogy** for orientation/gauge non-identifiability until a specific CR source category and symmetry functor are constructed.

## E. The honest completeness decision

Given a specified class `C` of sourced quantum models, let `U` collect all fixed allowed operational words, and let `≅_phys` denote a **separately specified** physical gauge/equivalence relation. A source-uniqueness property on `C` would require:

    U(M)=U(N) ⇒ M ≅_phys N, for all M,N in C.

This is **source rigidity on object-isomorphism classes**. If morphism reconstruction is claimed as well, the appropriate functor must be fully faithful on that specified class and essentially surjective onto the target operational category; *then* its ordinary category-theoretic quasi-inverse exists. Full faithfulness and source rigidity cannot be assumed as synonymous with the goal of P11 and counted as a derivation. Moreover a fully faithful functor by itself need not canonically pick the physically distinguished source absent actual premises that select a class of sources.
