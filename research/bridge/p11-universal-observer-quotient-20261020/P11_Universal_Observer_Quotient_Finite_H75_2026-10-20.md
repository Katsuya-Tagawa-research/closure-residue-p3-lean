## 1. Universal quotient theorem (EXACT finite linear algebra)

Let \(V\) be a real finite-dimensional vector space of Hermitian inputs (or any real vector space) and let \(C:V\to\mathbb R^m\) be the fixed catalog's full linear response evaluator, with \(r=\operatorname{rank}C\). Define
\[
N_C=\ker C,\qquad Q_C=V/N_C,\qquad
q_C:V\twoheadrightarrow Q_C,\qquad
\overline C([v])=C(v).
\]
Then \(\overline C:Q_C\hookrightarrow\mathbb R^m\) is well-defined and injective and \(C=\overline C\circ q_C\). Thus \(\dim Q_C=r\).

**Universal minimal predictor theorem:** For any *surjective linear encoding* \(q:V\twoheadrightarrow Z\), there exists a (necessarily unique) linear \(h:Z\to\mathbb R^m\) with \(C=h\circ q\) **iff**
\[
\ker q\subseteq \ker C.
\]
*Proof:* If \(C=hq\), then \(q(v)=0\) implies \(C(v)=0\). Conversely define \(h(q(v))=C(v)\); inclusion makes this well-defined, and surjectivity makes \(h\) unique. By rank–nullity, \(\dim Z\ge r\). Equality holds iff \(\ker q=\ker C\), and then the induced map \(Q_C\to Z\) is a uniquely determined isomorphism commuting with the quotient maps.

The claim is universal **among linear sufficient encodings for the declared catalog**; it does not assert that the quotient is itself a physical quantum Hilbert space, that \(\mathsf A\) was generated by F, or that any nonlinear compression has the same bound without further assumptions.

For an exact rational response matrix \(C\in\mathbb Q^{m\times n}\), choose \(r\) independent response rows as a coordinate matrix \(B\in\mathbb Q^{r\times n}\) using pivots of \(C^\top\). Then \(\ker B=\ker C\) and there is a *unique* rational \(D\in\mathbb Q^{m\times r}\) with
\[
\boxed{C=DB,\quad \operatorname{rank}B=r,\quad\operatorname{rank}D=r.}
\]
The basis \(B\) depends on the pivot convention; the quotient \(V/\ker C\) does **not**.

\[
\ker C_{\mathsf B}\subseteq\ker C_{\mathsf A}

\[

\qquad [v]_{\mathsf B}\mapsto[v]_{\mathsf A}

\[

\(\pi_{\mathsf C\mathsf A}=\pi_{\mathsf B\mathsf A}\pi_{\mathsf C\mathsf B}\).
